About University Resource Booking

Our mission is to make campus resource scheduling fast, simple, and accessible to everyone.

University Resource Booking is a collaborative project built to streamline the way students, faculty, and staff reserve study rooms, computer labs, and recreational facilities. Our platform is designed with simplicity and usability at its core.

We believe that booking a space should take seconds — not hours — and that transparency and availability should always be clear. Our system allows you to explore available spaces, check detailed information, and confirm reservations in just a few clicks.
